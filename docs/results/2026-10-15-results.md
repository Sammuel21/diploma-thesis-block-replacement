# Thesis results, snapshot 2026-10-15

Homogeneous SwiGLU replacement against MLP weight quantization on pinned
SmolLM2-1.7B. Only the MLPs of layers 1-22 change in either method. Metrics
follow [core-eval](../methodology/core-eval.md). Details live in the
experiment reports: [SwiGLU results](../experiments/model/swiglu/swiglu-results.md),
[SwiGLU-8](../experiments/model/swiglu/swiglu-8.md), and
[Quantization-1](../experiments/model/baseline/quantization-1.md).
Rows marked *to be measured* are placeholders.

## Headline

- **Size:** at matched bytes, quantization keeps far more quality. INT8 against
  SwiGLU 50% (both save 32%): KL 0.003 against 0.229. INT4 QAT against SwiGLU
  75% (48%): KL 0.035 against 0.471.
- **Size frontier:** quantization holds every frontier point up to 47.5%
  saving. Even INT4 without retraining beats every SwiGLU model at or below
  that budget, including 50% recovered for 1B tokens. SwiGLU appears on the
  frontier only at 75-80%, beyond the most aggressive quantized variant tested.
- **Computation:** SwiGLU removes up to 52% of linear MACs per token;
  weight-only quantization leaves the logical MAC count unchanged.
- **Speed (raw):** quantization gave no speedup; INT4 ran at dense speed and
  INT8 15-28% slower. SwiGLU speed is *to be measured*.
- **Within replacement:** local fitting still lowers 8K KL by 10-24% after
  100M recovery tokens, with the gap still shrinking.

## SwiGLU family: what each step settled

| Step | Settled |
| --- | --- |
| SwiGLU-1 | Teacher-neuron initialization drives the first quality gain; BI allocation did not beat uniform |
| SwiGLU-2 | Single-block KL selects the best per-layer widths |
| SwiGLU-3 | 393,216 calibration pairs; recovery keeps helping over long budgets |
| SwiGLU-4 | Recovery recipe: constant 3e-5, temperature-1 KL, replacement MLPs only |
| SwiGLU-5 | Discrete width allocation; gains persist to 100M |
| SwiGLU-6 | Recovery keeps improving to 1B, but does not reach dense |
| SwiGLU-7 | Replacement-only recovery beats wider retraining scopes at the lowest cost |
| SwiGLU-8 | Fitting recipe on a plateau; local fitting worth 10-24% KL at 100M; 20-80% spectrum |
